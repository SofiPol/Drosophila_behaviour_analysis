Methods reference

What is computed.

Sleep

Sleep is ≥5 consecutive minutes of immobility, assigned retroactively from the start of the run (Hendricks 2000; Shaw 2000). Bout categories: short ≤30 min, intermediate 30–60 min, long >60 min.

Circadian

Chi-square periodogram (Sokolove & Bushell 1978); Lomb–Scargle (Lomb 1976; Scargle 1982); autocorrelation and RI/RS (Levine et al. 2002); FFT; cosinor; onset regression.

Phase

IS and IV (van Someren 1999), M10/L5, relative amplitude, centre of gravity, acrophase, M/E peaks, day/night ratio and anticipation indices (Harrisingh 2007).

Statistics

Shapiro–Wilk, Welch/Student t, Mann–Whitney, one-way ANOVA, Kruskal–Wallis, Dunn, Fisher exact, chi-square, Holm/BH correction, Cohen's d and rank-biserial effect sizes.

Full formulas, parameter definitions and references are in METHODS.md in the repository.
